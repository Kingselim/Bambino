Track Your Pregnancy Trimester by Trimester

Each trimester of pregnancy brings new stages and challenges. Join us in this detailed journey to understand the changes in each trimester.

First Trimester

The first trimester is a period of major development for your baby and a time of significant changes for you. The baby develops from an embryo to a fetus with all major organs starting to form.

  • Changes in mother's body during the first trimester

Second Trimester

The second trimester often brings a resurgence of energy and may feel more comfortable than the first. Your baby's features begin to develop more distinctly, and you might start to feel movement.

  • Fetal development in the second trimester
  • Changes in mother's body during the second trimester

Third Trimester

This final trimester is the time to prepare for delivery. The baby's growth is rapid, and you might feel more discomfort as your body adjusts to accommodate the nearing birth.
