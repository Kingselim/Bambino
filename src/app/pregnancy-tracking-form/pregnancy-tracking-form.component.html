Track Your Pregnancy Month by Month

Each month of pregnancy has its own uniqueness! From the first to the ninth month, so much happens inside your belly, for you, and for your future baby! From nausea to childbirth, you will experience a fantastic adventure, full of twists and beautiful moments. With it comes the joy of becoming a parent, the joy of seeing your little one for the very first time. Join us in this beautiful journey with our month-by-month pregnancy guide!

First Trimester: Months 1 to 3

The first trimester, from week 1 to week 13, is quite an adventure. Expect vertigo, fatigue, nausea, ear pain, and abdominal pain. Don't worry, it's temporary and mostly harmless. This trimester is filled with medical appointments, blood tests, ultrasounds, and it's a secret to keep until the end of the third month!

Second Trimester: Months 4 to 6

The second trimester marks the phase between week 14 and week 26 of pregnancy. You should start to feel better day by day. Your belly will round out, and you'll gain some weight and feel the baby move. Radiant and blooming, you'll probably see your baby again during the second ultrasound. Boy or girl? Or... surprise?

Third Trimester: Months 7 to 9

The third trimester begins and ends your pregnancy but starts your journey into motherhood. These last few months may drag on, with discomforts affecting your mood and emotional state each day. It typically starts in the 27th week of pregnancy and ends around your expected delivery date, unless the baby decides to come earlier. Stay warm and get ready to meet your little one soon!
